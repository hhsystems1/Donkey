FOR THE GROWN-UPS · GEAR GUIDE

Good gear starts
with good questions.

Compare brands, check the evidence, and find the right fit for your rider.

A research shortlist, ready for a closer look.

These are candidates under review, not approved recommendations. There are no affiliate links here. Manufacturer information is a starting point; fit, exact model, recalls, and independent evidence still need checking.

Start with the helmet.

A brand name can’t tell you whether a particular helmet fits your child or their riding activity.

01
CHILDREN’S BICYCLE HELMET

woom READY

A children’s option with several head-size ranges to compare.

Evidence & checks still needed

The manufacturer lists XS, S, and M sizes. Measure your rider’s head and confirm the label on the exact U.S. model. Don’t assume suitability for an e-bike from its appearance.

Before shortlisting: check certification, actual fit, current recalls, and any independent rating for the exact model.

01
YOUTH KNEE PADS

Fox Youth Launch

A youth option with D3O inserts.

Evidence & checks still needed

Fox lists EN 1621-1 Level 1 protection. That describes a protection test; it does not guarantee injury prevention.

Before shortlisting: confirm the exact current model, youth sizing, strap comfort, freedom of movement, and whether the pad stays over the knee.

How a candidate becomes a recommendation
  1. Identify the exact product and intended rider or activity.
  2. Verify applicable certification and manufacturer instructions.
  3. Check independent evidence where available, plus current recalls.
  4. Assess fit, compatibility, authorized sellers, and service.
  5. Record the review date and limits before adding any purchase link.
