Ride Smart · Pre-ride check

30 seconds. Every ride.

  1. Helmet: level, straps in a V under each ear, snug buckle.
  2. Brakes: squeeze both. The bike should not roll.
  3. Tires: press them. Firm, no cuts or bulges.
  4. Battery: clicked in, charged, no damage or smell.
  5. Lights and bell: they work, front and back.
  6. Shoes and clothes: closed shoes, laces tucked, nothing loose by the chain.
  7. Plan: tell a grown-up where you're going and when you'll be back.

Something fails? Don't ride. Tell a grown-up.
