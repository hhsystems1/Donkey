Privacy

Plain-English privacy.

Last updated November 4, 2026.

Kids and the course

The lessons are made for kids, so they collect nothing. No accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no affiliate links. Progress and the rider's first name are saved only on the device they use, and never sent to us. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child sent us something, email us and we'll delete it.

What we collect from parents

Only what you type in: your email, and if you choose, your rider's first name, age and your state. If you take the readiness check, we also keep your score and the topics to work on, so the email plan matches. If you order a certificate, we keep the name to print, the completion date and your order.

What we use it for

To send the course link, your plan, a short series of safety emails, and the certificate. We don't sell your information. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

Who helps us

Our email provider sends the emails, our customer system stores your sign-up, and our payment processor handles certificate payments. They only use your information to do that work for us.

Gear links

Some links on the parent site are affiliate links. When you click one, the store may set its own cookie so it can credit us if you buy. We count clicks on those links, with no personal information attached.

Deleting your information
