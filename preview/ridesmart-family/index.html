How it works

Check. Learn. Ride.

  1. Check

    Answer seven questions with your rider. You get a plan with only what they're missing, plus your state's rules.

  2. Learn

    They take six short lessons on their own. Every card is read out loud, so early readers can keep up. About 30 minutes total.

  3. Ride

    A final check, the rider pledge, and a certificate. You do the pre-ride check together before every ride.

Questions

Before you start.

Is the course really free?

Yes. All six lessons are free. The only thing you can pay for is the official certificate and rider card, and that's optional. We also earn small commissions on some gear we recommend, at no cost to you.

Can my 7-year-old do it alone?

Yes. Every card is read out loud, and answers are taps, not typing. For the first lesson, sitting next to them helps a lot.

Do you collect anything from my kid?

No. There are no accounts in the course, no tracking, and no ads. Progress is saved only on the device they use. We only collect your email, if you choose to give it.

Is my kid old enough for an e-bike?

It depends on the rider, the bike and your state. Some states set age limits by e-bike class. The readiness check and the state law lookup are the place to start.

Is this legal advice?

No. It's general safety education. Rules change by state and town, so check yours before they ride.
